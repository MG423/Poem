ICE BENEATH WATER

Everything felt so perfect,

Like it was meant to be.

With all the fun I had,

And the ones you had,

Pretentiously or legitimately,

It felt like ice submerging in water.

Like the Titanic never sank,

Like the Bumblebee never flew,

And,

A Love Story,

That ceased to exist.

Mehul Ghosh
