THE GAZE

The wind was gentle,

The sun was soft orange;

Two tired souls were next to each other,

They locked eyes,

Her eyes were dreamy,

Enchanting and impossible to look away from,

As though they held another world within,

He felt like diving into those ocean-blue eyes.

That moment felt like a lifetime,

A lifetime that could go on forever,

A gaze that could heal every pain.

A gaze that can make his day.

A gaze to die for.

His hands were shaking,

With panic.

His pupils were dilating,

With awe.

His face was red,

With shyness.

His heart was pounding,

With immense love.

And in that moment

He fell in love again.

Mehul Ghosh
